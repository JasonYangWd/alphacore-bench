One ESP32 hub, six pluggable edges. Drop as many modules on an edge as fit its 4 GPIO pins — or build a multi-part module in the editor.

Toolbox

Drag a part onto any edge, or build a composite module first. Each edge holds up to 4 GPIO pins' worth of parts.

AlphaCore Edges

All 6 hexagon edges are wired identically on the real AlphaCore board (ESP32-S3): power plus 4 GPIO pins — 3 are ADC-capable (DO/DI/PWM/ADC), 1 is digital-only (DO/DI/PWM, no ADC) — and every edge also reaches the shared I2C bus, which doesn't use up any of the 4 pins.

Notebook

Write setup() and loop() for the master. Only modules attached to an edge are available on the right — drag a call into the code, or just click to copy it in.

Module Editor

Combine parts into one composite module (up to 4 GPIO pins total, of which at most 3 can need ADC; I2C parts don't count against that budget), then save it to the toolbox.
